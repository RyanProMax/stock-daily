# Complete, verified daily recap

When a market closes, the scheduled report must investigate a broad benchmark, every one of its 11 industry sectors, and all 8 displayed AI baskets. Coverage is determined from collected instruments, never from a model's selection.

For each target the report must explain direction, transmission and the size of the move, distinguish verified facts from interpretation, cite verified original passages, test competing explanations, and state what would invalidate the interpretation. A daily close change is not an intraday trading range. Neither a basket average nor four representative stocks establishes index contribution or total-market breadth.

Every run must attempt the registered public X/KOL source set. Original author and post timestamps must be attributable; unavailable access is recorded honestly. Social opinions are research leads and attributed perspectives, never substitutes for authoritative factual evidence and never extra independent corroboration of the same syndicated story.

Every reader claim and number must survive deterministic source/time/excerpt checks and an isolated semantic review. An accessible URL or a reputable domain alone does not verify a claim. Missing evidence produces a recorded gap, not an invented cause. A market cannot publish or be marked completed until all required explanations pass review and its exact content is read back from production.

Checkpoints must preserve inputs, queries, source hashes, outputs, reviews and failures. Retry costs remain visible. The previous valid production slice is retained on incomplete coverage. Evidence and interpretation live inside their corresponding market/sector/basket, with localized dates and complete mobile layouts.

Acceptance: deterministic 20-target coverage; adversarial evidence tests; real X preflight; frozen-session non-publishing end-to-end run; 1440px/390px full-page and all-expanded screenshot review; publication/readback tests. Live end-to-end failures and unverified explanations must never be reported as acceptance successes.
