# Design

The proposed qualified-inference path remains pending implementation and live acceptance. It must retain separate source IDs for a fresh official root event, independently reported contemporaneous reaction to that same event, and target-specific first-party structural transmission evidence. A macro passage cannot be relabeled as a direct sector quote. All required basket subjects must be supported, or the claim must stay explicitly limited to the supported constituents. The claimed direction must address the actual observation and contrary forces: for example, rising long yields alone cannot complete an explanation of a rising utilities ETF. Inferences must be clearly identified in both languages, capped at medium confidence, and pass independent review for every link, chronology, counterevidence and magnitude. Missing roots/transmission or measured prices alone cannot become a complete explanation. Shared originals count as one origin. This design does not alter the current complete-attribution gate or certify a real report.

Collect structural transmission evidence separately as first-party background disclosures in comprehensive research. Independently check their original date, target/constituent identity and scope; restrict them to the 400-day period before the window start. Date-only background must be from an earlier calendar day. The exact opening SEC archival As filed header establishes a filing date, not an invented clock time or a prospectus effective date. A company disclosure supports only code-identified constituents. Background records do not satisfy the fresh official-event gate, supply a current price reaction or independently complete attribution; ordinary headline research cannot admit them. This separation provides the basis for a subsequently reviewed inference path without relabeling background as a new event.

Magnitude has two separate admissible evidence paths. Reported opposing forces can use verified target-specific passages. Measured net-move explanations must cite typed observation IDs: the actual instrument's opening-gap/open-to-close components plus its independently observed intraday range, or an explicitly equal-weight basket's positive/negative arithmetic contributions. Validation requires those measurements and units in both language versions, rejects cross-target/unknown IDs and rederives everything from the frozen input at publication. Optional prior-twenty-session range/volume ratios cannot independently complete magnitude coverage. Measurements can explain mechanical cancellation and constrain economic interpretation; they never prove a catalyst or substitute for official facts, direct target-causal evidence or independent semantic review.

Search seeds name the two largest observed representative moves as leads; this ordering is frozen arithmetic, not a claim that those companies caused the sector's return. Verify each four-target batch before starting the next. Feed confirmed access refusals to later batches; two distinct refused article URLs are required before excluding a publisher. Persist each batch's access hints so resuming reproduces its exact research input and prompt hash. A missing timestamp or unsupported claim cannot blacklist a whole publisher as inaccessible.

Manual publication and standard check/readback commands require the same complete-recap approval as scheduled publication. Legacy structure validation cannot be used as full-sector acceptance. Current sector quote links name the provider actually read; an unread official index or exchange page cannot lend its authority to a third-party quotation.

Retain the existing isolated headline research and publication recovery. Add a deterministic comprehensive target plan and independent facts/causal research for each required target, bounded to four concurrent targets (eight workers). Give that research its own persisted ledger, and increase the query ceiling by exactly four queries per required target. The comprehensive path allows 35 minutes of research and 55 minutes overall per market; exhaustion fails safely rather than shrinking coverage. The two-market wrapper stops after 115 minutes. Initial live timings demonstrated that the older two-target budget cannot cover 20 targets.

After verification, a dedicated writer produces bilingual direction/magnitude explanations against evidence IDs. A separate reviewer checks every target and each evidence assignment. Deterministic gates enforce exact coverage, source provenance, numbers, competing forces and review scores. Both the approved recap and its ledger enter the immutable publication audit; the recap participates in market content hashes and inactive-market preservation.

Freeze original daily bars separately from news. CSI supplies China industry bars, Tencent supplies Shanghai's broad benchmark, and Yahoo supplies available equity/ETF bars. Recompute the same-session return against the previous session and reject quotes inconsistent with collected values. Derive range, opening-gap and intraday components with the previous-close denominator, and compare volume/range with the preceding twenty sessions only. Revalidate raw-payload hashes and every derived value again before publication. Closing-return observations reconstruct the four-stock equal-weight AI baskets and their positive/negative offsets; they do not supply weighted sector contribution or synthetic basket high/low. Measurements constrain narratives but never substitute for evidence of a catalyst.

X originals are collected once as a frozen input. Author, ID and time are checked against the allowlist and post ID; text is a discovered original-source lead, not independently confirmed factual truth. Writer can add attributed perspectives only when facts are corroborated by its authoritative ledger. Missing access remains an explicit report-state limitation.

For the pinned twscrape 0.20.0 runtime, locally apply the reviewed upstream PR #328 compatibility fix: legacy script hashes can be seven or sixteen hexadecimal characters. Preserve modern-bundle/logged-out checks; do not edit the installed dependency, credentials or challenge handling. Require both authentication cookies before declaring an account ready. Capture only sanitized error categories from swallowed library errors so parser failures cannot masquerade as an absence of posts.

Freeze Treasury's original annual XML separately from timestamped news. Validate HTTPS authority, exact series/query, namespaces, dates, yields, revision time and original-text hash. Recompute the 2/10/30-year changes and 10-minus-2 spread in basis points. US requires the exact current and previous session; China uses the latest US observation before China's session date. Exclude future observations, avoid forward fills, and verify cross-year records independently. Observation dates are not article publication timestamps, and the retrieved feed revision cannot establish a historical publication vintage. Treasury interpolated par yields and a news quote of an on-the-run bond at another time are distinct measurements. Share this frozen context with both researchers and the writer/reviewer as a hypothesis and consistency input; it does not independently establish a sector catalyst or bypass the cited-evidence gate. Revalidate this artifact and its derived values before publication and include it in recoverable latest-run artifacts.

Reader data stores compact explanations and source links, not full articles, prompts or internal review scores. The UI attaches disclosures to individual sector/basket cells; open cells span the grid width and stack normally on mobile.
