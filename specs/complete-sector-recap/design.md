# Design

For US sector exposure, the reviewed SEC filing URL and eleven fund risk headings form a deterministic background seed. Freeze its unmodified original HTML/hash once, establish the exact original filing date, and extract complete concentration and economic-factor sentences without clipping at page footers. When necessary, use the fund code actually printed between the sentences; do not generate it into a quotation. The same frozen body supplies source verification and publication re-extraction. A missing, wrong-authority, undated or more-than-400-day-old filing supplies gaps and leaves the independent research path available; it cannot be silently treated as a fresh event. This seed does not supply China or AI company exposures.

The qualified-inference path is implemented but still needs real complete-report acceptance. A separate isolated attribution process selects chains before writing; the independent final reviewer challenges each link. Each chain retains separate original IDs for a fresh official root, an independent contemporary reaction to that same event, and target-specific first-party structural transmission evidence. Exact shared event anchors and chronology are checked in code; semantic identity and economic sign still require independent review. A same-day date-only root also needs its specific event value confirmed in a timed report before close. A later wrap cannot fill that timing gap. A macro quote cannot become a direct sector quote. It may support a qualified common-factor inference without naming the sector when the separate first-party exposure establishes the target's intervening mechanism and defensible sign; absence of a target name alone cannot reject this branch. A generic risk list cannot supply that sign, and a possible contribution cannot become proven dominance. All four basket subjects must be covered unless an admissible industry authority establishes the entire layer. The direction must match the actual observation and address contrary forces: rising long yields alone cannot explain a rising utilities ETF. Inferences must be identified in both languages, capped at medium confidence, and preserve all selected chain IDs. Missing roots/transmission, prices alone, or unresolved targets cannot complete publication. The chain artifact/hash and its isolated process output are rechecked at publication and retained with the recoverable latest-run artifacts. These gates do not certify a real report by themselves.

One bounded evidence-repair pass follows the first complete research/attribution assessment. Every target already received the fixed initial searches; remaining capacity selects unresolved targets by descending research priority and original-input tie order, retaining zero-return targets. Priority is the larger of the absolute net close and, only for an explicit four-stock equal-weight basket, the average absolute returns of companies still missing approved chains. This private arithmetic keeps large offsetting moves visible; it is neither a measured causal contribution nor reader-facing confidence. Each selected target consumes a complete isolated facts/causal pair of four fresh queries, at most twelve supplementary queries inside the original cumulative query/time budgets. The plan, assessment and shared-evidence/access context bind a stable input hash and remain independently reconstructible on resume. Exact process fingerprints decide reusable queries; completed flags cannot replenish capacity. Mixed source ledgers reuse only frozen-original-reaudited positives while reconsidering refusals, preserving accepted proof timestamps and repair payloads. New evidence is verified before a new isolated attribution assessment. It does not force a passing verdict or let incomplete coverage enter writing/publication.

Current company passages retain a single supplied constituent symbol and their original scope. Code locates that company in the unchanged excerpt and checks native issuer ownership or SEC archive/inline issuer identity; company labels and subjects are rederived during frozen-source replay. A competitor mention cannot supply the issuer identity or broaden a company event to its basket. The v2 attribution artifact permits up to eight component/whole-target chains for an explicitly equal-weight four-stock AI basket, with an explicit reported/inferred basis and component subjects per chain. Each company must have a chain supporting its own actual sign (opposing forces or a justified flat mechanism for zero), and the same-session arithmetic must reproduce the basket within rounding tolerance. Inferred links retain their own first-party exposures and make the entire entry medium-capped; reported high confidence requires two independent identified reaction origins per company. Both writer languages and the independent final reviewer retain each company's specific causes. An unresolved basket can preserve admissible company chains privately for targeted repair but still fails complete publication. Unknown sector weights preclude this route for ETFs.

Nonnumeric event anchors must be literal contiguous wording shared by both original excerpts. A company alias, assembled descriptive label or a forecast value absent from the reaction cannot stand in for a shared phrase. The verifier names the target, root/reaction IDs and rejected anchors in deterministic repair feedback; matching words still require an independent semantic assessment of the same event.

Collect structural transmission evidence separately as first-party background disclosures in comprehensive research. Independently check their original date, target/constituent identity and scope; restrict them to the 400-day period before the window start. Date-only background must be from an earlier calendar day. The exact opening SEC archival As filed header establishes a filing date, not an invented clock time or a prospectus effective date. A company disclosure supports only code-identified constituents. Background records do not satisfy the fresh official-event gate, supply a current price reaction or independently complete attribution; ordinary headline research cannot admit them. This separation provides the basis for a subsequently reviewed inference path without relabeling background as a new event.

Company exposure extraction segments the whitespace-normalized complete original text before selecting sentences, so printed line breaks and page footers cannot produce sentence tails missing their subject. Selected local context includes the preceding sentence when it fits, explicit following qualifications and recognized same-topic rate-risk continuations. A qualification that cannot fit the excerpt allowance rejects that candidate rather than being cut off. Risk-free assumptions in stock-option/PSU or lease accounting do not become economic risk; actual disclosed sensitivities and denials remain visible. Rate-specific exposures receive research precedence over generic funding references; explicit denials of sensitivity and hypothetical decreases in interest expense remain available as contrary evidence, without assigning a causal score. At most three distinct nonoverlapping contexts remain per issuer, and their exact words/numbers/order are reverified against the unchanged original; these are discovery ingredients, not signed daily-cause approvals.

Current company-target and historical company-target records retain only the exact supplied member after original issuer matching; shared AI membership does not widen the original source. Background factor recognition covers credit/capital markets and borrowing as well as explicitly named financing. It does not turn background into a current event or certify economic sign. Reopened snapshots retain their already verified publication receipts and original fetch time, allowing recollection to preserve the SEC proof rather than silently dropping it. Per-collection extraction reuse binds the complete original snapshot hash, including its bytes and timing receipts; changes reopen it, and independent validation always uses a fresh cache.

Magnitude has two separate admissible evidence paths. Reported opposing forces can use verified target-specific passages. Measured net-move explanations must cite typed observation IDs: the actual instrument's opening-gap/open-to-close components plus its independently observed intraday range, or an explicitly equal-weight basket's positive/negative arithmetic contributions. Validation requires those measurements and units in both language versions, rejects cross-target/unknown IDs and rederives everything from the frozen input at publication. Optional prior-twenty-session range/volume ratios cannot independently complete magnitude coverage. Measurements can explain mechanical cancellation and constrain economic interpretation; they never prove a catalyst or substitute for official facts, direct target-causal evidence or independent semantic review.

Search seeds name the two largest observed representative moves as leads; this ordering is frozen arithmetic, not a claim that those companies caused the sector's return. Verify each four-target batch before starting the next. Freeze the verified prior-batch originals as shared research leads, preserving their IDs/scopes, so subsequent work can focus on missing exposure or counterevents. Sharing does not retag or certify a new sector cause. Feed confirmed access refusals to later batches; two distinct refused article URLs are required before excluding a publisher. Persist each batch's evidence and access hints so resuming reproduces its exact input/prompt hash. Research execution, checkpoint repair and capacity checks use the same payload builder, including macro and shared evidence. A missing timestamp or unsupported claim cannot blacklist a whole publisher as inaccessible.

Manual publication and standard check/readback commands require the same complete-recap approval as scheduled publication. Legacy structure validation cannot be used as full-sector acceptance. Current sector quote links name the provider actually read; an unread official index or exchange page cannot lend its authority to a third-party quotation.

Retain the existing isolated headline research and publication recovery. Add a deterministic comprehensive target plan and independent facts/causal research for each required target, bounded to four concurrent targets (eight workers). Give that research its own persisted ledger, and increase the query ceiling by exactly four queries per required target. The comprehensive path allows 35 minutes of research and 55 minutes overall per market; exhaustion fails safely rather than shrinking coverage. The two-market wrapper stops after 115 minutes. Initial live timings demonstrated that the older two-target budget cannot cover 20 targets.

After verification, a dedicated writer produces bilingual direction/magnitude explanations against evidence IDs. A separate reviewer checks every target and each evidence assignment. Deterministic gates enforce exact coverage, source provenance, numbers, competing forces and review scores. Both the approved recap and its ledger enter the immutable publication audit; the recap participates in market content hashes and inactive-market preservation.

Freeze original daily bars separately from news. CSI supplies China industry bars, Tencent supplies Shanghai's broad benchmark, and Yahoo supplies available equity/ETF bars. Recompute the same-session return against the previous session and reject quotes inconsistent with collected values. Derive range, opening-gap and intraday components with the previous-close denominator, and compare volume/range with the preceding twenty sessions only. Revalidate raw-payload hashes and every derived value again before publication. Closing-return observations reconstruct the four-stock equal-weight AI baskets and their positive/negative offsets; they do not supply weighted sector contribution or synthetic basket high/low. Measurements constrain narratives but never substitute for evidence of a catalyst.

X originals are collected once as a frozen input. Author, ID and time are checked against the allowlist and post ID; text is a discovered original-source lead, not independently confirmed factual truth. Writer can add attributed perspectives only when facts are corroborated by its authoritative ledger. Missing access remains an explicit report-state limitation.

For the pinned twscrape 0.20.0 runtime, locally apply the reviewed upstream PR #328 compatibility fix: legacy script hashes can be seven or sixteen hexadecimal characters. Preserve modern-bundle/logged-out checks; do not edit the installed dependency, credentials or challenge handling. Require both authentication cookies before declaring an account ready. Capture only sanitized error categories from swallowed library errors so parser failures cannot masquerade as an absence of posts.

Freeze Treasury's original annual XML separately from timestamped news. Validate HTTPS authority, exact series/query, namespaces, dates, yields, revision time and original-text hash. Recompute the 2/10/30-year changes and 10-minus-2 spread in basis points. US requires the exact current and previous session; China uses the latest US observation before China's session date. Exclude future observations, avoid forward fills, and verify cross-year records independently. Observation dates are not article publication timestamps, and the retrieved feed revision cannot establish a historical publication vintage. Treasury interpolated par yields and a news quote of an on-the-run bond at another time are distinct measurements. Share this frozen context with both researchers and the writer/reviewer as a hypothesis and consistency input; it does not independently establish a sector catalyst or bypass the cited-evidence gate. Revalidate this artifact and its derived values before publication and include it in recoverable latest-run artifacts.

Reader data stores compact explanations and source links, not full articles, prompts or internal review scores. The UI attaches disclosures to individual sector/basket cells; open cells span the grid width and stack normally on mobile.
